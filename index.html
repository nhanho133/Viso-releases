Questions, answered

ViSO có gửi âm thanh hay dữ liệu của tôi lên cloud không?

Không. ViSO nghe và hiểu ngay trên máy bạn, không cần mạng. Ghi chú nằm trong máy bạn; muốn sao lưu thì tự xuất ra một file và giữ ở đâu tuỳ bạn.

Tôi có thể gõ phím thay vì nói không?

Có. Trong khung ViSO trên trang web hay bảng Notes của app, gõ trực tiếp; gõ @ để chọn loại (ghi chú, lời nhắc, việc) thay vì phải nói đúng từ khoá.

Manifesto

Most productivity tools create more friction than they solve.

Every time inspiration strikes or a task pops up, you are forced to:

  • 1 Stop your current work.
  • 2 Open Notion, Todoist, or Apple Notes.
  • 3 Decide whether it's a note, a task, a reminder, or an event.
  • 4 Lose 15 minutes of your deep focus (Flow State) trying to organize it.

We believe your computer should adapt to how your brain works, not the other way around.

Privacy, in one screen

ViSO is local-first. Concretely:

  • Speech recognition and intent parsing run on your CPU (whisper.cpp, zipformer). No audio is uploaded, ever.
  • Notes, tasks and reminders live in a SQLite database in your user profile, and in the Chrome extension's own storage. Backups are JSON files you download yourself.
  • The extension reads the page you're on only to know where a note belongs — a title, an address, a favicon. It never sends page content anywhere.
  • The one download is the search model (multilingual-e5-small, 118 MB), fetched once from Hugging Face and verified against a pinned checksum.
  • No account, no telemetry, no analytics in the Alpha. The waitlist email is the only thing you give us.
